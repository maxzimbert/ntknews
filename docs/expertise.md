# Expertise — the pillar, the problem, and the build

**Verified against the repo 2026-10-23** (T-0100 merged; T-0101 adds firsts and marks). This is the
entry point for Expertise the way `docs/backstory.md` is for Backstory. The vision lives in
`~/Desktop/NTK/Briefings and narratives/ntk-expertise-narrative-v1.1.html` (the briefing);
the mechanism lives in `digest/index.html`; the open work is in `docs/tickets/`. Where this
file and the code disagree, the code wins. `features/expertise_xp.md` on the Desktop is an
older XP-and-points spec that the briefing superseded; do not build from it.

---

## 1. The pitch, in sixty seconds

NTK keeps a private record of what you pay attention to. Not points, not a daily reset, no
streak to protect. Every subject you read becomes a **beat**, and there are seven words for
how far you have gone on it: Skimmed, Briefed, Read It, Following It, On the Beat, Carrying
It, Witness. The early words say what you did to a story. The late words say what you are
doing with a subject. You cannot skim your way to Witness: it takes coming back, reading the
Lies layer, going into the Backstory, and passing a story on.

The system never asks you to do anything. It notices what you already did and says so, once,
between stories, in NTK's voice. Your record lives on your device. `/me` is the portrait:
what kind of reader you are, and what you have been paying attention to.

Three systems, one arc. **The Beat** (live) is what you know. **The Triforce** (next) is who
you have become: Curiosity, Persistence, Assuredness, reader-wide, unlocked by your first
Witness. **The Pyramid of Success** (horizon) is who you can be. Only the first is built.

Comparables, and the difference: Yahoo Sports Rewards and Milestones are points redeemable
outside the app. NYT Spelling Bee has a clear rank ladder with visible thresholds, which is
the part worth borrowing, and a daily reset, which is not. NTK's record accumulates for life,
is never redeemed, and recognises behaviour rather than manufacturing it.

## 2. The problem, measured 2026-10-21 before this change

The editor named three parts. All three were true.

**You could not inspect it.** The only way to see rung 5 was to read as a reader for seven
weeks. No fixture reader, no way to step through states, no page that listed every line the
system can say. The founder had never seen most of the product's own copy in place.

**Prod fell short of the briefing in specific ways.**

- Backstory fed nothing. The briefing calls the History layer the byway and weights it
  heavily; `showBsrPage` recorded an analytics event and nothing else. The Witness toast
  said "you went into the history" to readers who never had.
- Links fed nothing. A source followed out of a story, a primary document opened from an
  object page, a local headline on Today: none of it was recorded anywhere.
- Shares were credited on the tap, before the share sheet opened, cancelled or not. Copy
  link counted the same as a sent message.
- Nothing was reader-wide. Finishing a digest left no trace (T-0021). There was no "what
  kind of reader am I", only per-subject rungs.
- `scripts/beats-sim.js`, the one tool for seeing the ladder over time, had been throwing
  `BEAT_REQS is not defined` since 2026-09-18, when T-0025 made `beatIdx` table-driven.
  Nobody noticed because nobody ran it.
- The Triforce, the Witness graduation moment, the Map, the Witness Report: not built.

**You could not pitch or demo it.** The chip was one unexplained word. `/me` was a list of
cards with a bar. No screen anywhere said what the pillar is, and there was no way to put a
phone in someone's hand at the interesting state.

## 3. What this change builds (T-0100)

All in `digest/index.html` unless noted.

- **Inspect mode.** `/me?inspect=<reader>` runs the whole app as a fixture reader. Readers:
  `new`, `day2`, `week2`, `month2`. An amber bar switches between them, **Every line** opens
  a sheet listing every piece of copy the system can say, generated by calling the same
  functions production calls, and **Reset** reseeds. Everything is written to a second set
  of storage keys (`__inspect` suffix, see `nsKey`), so the editor's own record is never
  touched, and `track()` is silent. Add `&reset` to a URL to reseed by hand.
- **Backstory feeds the ladder.** A row page is credited to the subject of the story it is
  paired with today, once per row per sitting (`beatsRecordHistory`). Objects count as
  texture. If the trip crosses a rung, the ladder speaks on the way back to the tab.
  **Witness now requires two trips into the Backstory** (`history: 2` in `BEAT_REQS`). The
  lower rungs are unchanged, so a reader who never opens the tab still reaches Carrying It.
- **Links.** Outbound links from a story, a Backstory page or Today are recorded
  (`beatsRecordLink`, `recordBump`). Texture, not status.
- **Shares count on success.** `navigator.share` resolves when the sheet reports the share
  went somewhere; only then is it a share. Copy link is kept apart as `copies`. The web
  cannot see which app a share went to; iMessage, Instagram and email are one count until
  the app is native.
- **The record.** `ntk_record_v1` holds one dated entry per finished edition and reader-wide
  counts. `ntk_visits_v1` now keeps `first`. `/me` opens with the record as a line of
  counts: reading since, digests finished, subjects followed, Lies layers, Backstory trips,
  documents opened, sources followed, stories passed on. Counts of things done, never of
  days. No calendar, no gap, no streak.
- **`/me` rebuilt.** Each beat is a card with a seven-stop rail in the rung's colour, the
  five behaviours it is made of marked done or not, and the clippings. Tapping a card opens
  the explainer with the rung's meaning and what the next one takes. This is option A below.
- `scripts/beats-sim.js` runs again and models Backstory trips.

### Added by T-0101 (2026-10-23)

- **Firsts and marks.** Ten one-time moments, each fired once ever (`MARKS`, `markFire`):
  first, fifth, tenth, 25th and 50th digest finished; first trip into the Backstory; first
  document read; back to the Backstory on a different day; first story passed on; first
  Backstory passed on. Toasts queue instead of overwriting. Each appears on `/me` under the
  record with the day it happened. A first-share mark yields to the Carrying It toast when
  both land on one close.
- **The Moment of Then carries the record line**: "Your 14th digest, start to finish. More
  tomorrow." It replaced a cheer with an em dash in it. The digest marks fire there, silently.
- **Backstory pages can be shared.** A Share control on the row page; credited to the paired
  subject like a story share, with the same success-only rule.
- **The voice rule**, applied to every line: a concrete thing the reader did, past tense, then
  the word for it. Nothing about the reader's character. "That is attention, not habit" and
  "you cannot unsee it" are gone. The next-rung sentence is nouns, not instructions:
  "Carrying It takes 7 stories, 3 separate sittings, the Lies layer and either a share or the
  Lies layer on 3 of them on one subject."
- Deliberately not a mark: turning on location for local news and weather. A permission grant
  is not reading, and rewarding one reads as a dark pattern.

## 5. A decision the editor has not made: how legible is status?

`docs/decisions.md` and the briefing say no points and no scoreboard. The editor cites
Spelling Bee, which shows a rank ladder with visible thresholds. The canvas shows three
options side by side.

| Option | What the card shows | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Legible, not numeric** | The rail, and which of five behaviours are done or not. Thresholds one tap away. | Reads as a system without becoming a quest log. Shipped. |
| **B · Spelling Bee** | Counts toward the next rung on the card: 5 of 7 stories, 2 of 3 Lies layers. | Clearest. Also an instruction, which the briefing says never to issue. Reopens the decision. |
| **C · Quiet** | Status word and the record. No rail, no marks, no next rung. | Truest to "a portrait, not a scoreboard." Nobody can tell it is a system (the T-0025 complaint). |

Prod ships A until the editor picks. Switching to B or C is a change to `renderProfileTab`
and nothing else.

## 6. What counts

| Behaviour | Recorded as | Status or texture |
|---|---|---|
| Opened a story | `stories`, `sessions` | status |
| Opened a section below the summary | `depth` | status |
| Revealed the Lies layer | `liesRead`, `liesStories[]` | status |
| Came back in a later sitting (4h) | `sessions` | status |
| Share sheet reported success | `shares` | status |
| Opened a Backstory row (per row, per sitting) | `history` | status (Witness) |
| Opened a Backstory object | `objects` | texture |
| Followed a link out | `links` (subject or record) | texture |
| Copied the link | `copies` | texture |
| Finished the digest | `record.editions[date]` | record |
| Entity on a read story | `entities{}` | clippings |
| A first (ten kinds, see `MARKS`) | `ntk_marks_v1[id]` = when | mark on /me, one toast |

Not observable from a web app, and said so rather than faked: which app a share went to.
Not counted yet, by choice: listening to a story, time on page, scroll depth.

## 7. How to demo it

1. Open `ntknews.org/me?inspect=month2`. The record line, five beats from Witness down to
   Briefed, the rail, the marks. Tap Executive Power: the explainer.
2. Switch to **week2** in the bar. Tap the chip on Digest: first the explanation, then what
   comes next.
3. **Every line**: the full copy of the system in one scroll.
4. Switch to **new**. Read three stories: the orientation card. Close a story after opening a
   section: the Briefed toast. Open Backstory, open a row, come back.
5. **Exit** returns to your own record, untouched.

Locally (`node scripts/preview.js`), the app is at `/digest/?inspect=month2`; `/me` is a
Netlify rule and does not exist on the preview server.
