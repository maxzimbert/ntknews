## What

The editor's plan is to carry the Hofstadter matrix (widened under T-0060)
into the near-present by drawing on Krause and Zelizer and on their critics.
This ticket is the identification step: name the issues each scholar raises
and the voices they and their critics bring in, and map each to an NTK row,
before anything is added to the matrix.

but is institutional. This ticket is about adding the scholarly and critical
layer, not creating the extension from nothing.

## Why

The editor's reasoning: the historical canon ends around 1981, and the
interpretive literature since then is what carries a reader up to today's
story. Ruled out: summarising the scholars from recall. A Backstory that
misstates a scholar's argument is the failure mode this project has already
paid for (T-0045, T-0047). Each entry needs a real source in hand, and the
critics need to be represented as critics, not folded into the argument they
dispute, in keeping with NTK's Truths / Probabilities / Possibilities / Lies
discipline. Output feeds T-0062. Depends on T-0060 only for shared columns,
not for sequencing.
