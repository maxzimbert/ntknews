## What

The editor's plan is to carry the Hofstadter matrix (widened under T-0060)
into the near-present with *Fault Lines: A History of the United States Since
1974* by Kevin M. Kruse and Julian E. Zelizer (Princeton historians), and with
their critics. This ticket is the identification step: name the issues, the
works and the voices, and map each to an NTK row, before anything enters the
matrix.

The editor's study guide is committed at
`editorial/research/american_politics_fault_lines.md` (read 2026-10-01). It
frames the book as four fault lines, which I propose mapping like this
(my mapping, for the editor to change):

| Fault line (per the guide) | Proposed NTK row |
|---|---|
| Party realignment, partisan sorting | Government, Power |
| Economic disparity, class divergence | Work |
| Cultural and social revolutions (gender, race, religion, Roe, busing) | Family, Faith, Equality |
| A fractured media landscape | The Media |

It also lists three critiques of the book (anti-racist/left-progressive:
"false nostalgia" and "both-sides"; conservative/MAGA: asymmetric blame and an
elite blind spot; Keynesian/political economy: culture over capital) and three
complementary works: Mann and Ornstein, *It's Even Worse Than It Looks*;
James and Deborah Fallows, *Our Towns*; Levitsky and Ziblatt, *How Democracies
Die* (the guide titles it *Why Democracies Die*; the title needs checking).

**Unverified, and the main risk:** the guide itself is a study guide, not a
source. It describes the three critiques as positions, with no named critic,
review or page behind any of them. It also dates the book to 2019 and
describes a revised edition with an "American Carnage" chapter and a 2024
epilogue; I have not checked either. Nothing from the guide should enter
`backstory.json` until each claim has a real source in hand.

State of the matrix today (measured 2026-10-01): 90 objects are already
tagged "Extension 1981-2026", mostly government documents (Public Papers of
the Presidents, Dept. of State, Heritage Foundation). So the extension exists
but is institutional. This ticket adds the scholarly and critical layer.

## Why

The editor's reasoning: the historical canon ends around 1981, and the
interpretive literature since then is what carries a reader up to today's
story. Ruled out: summarising the scholars from recall. A Backstory that
misstates a scholar's argument is the failure mode this project has already
paid for (T-0045, T-0047). Each entry needs a real source in hand. The critics
must appear as critics, with a named person and a published piece, not folded
into the argument they dispute, in keeping with NTK's Truths / Probabilities /
Possibilities / Lies discipline. The guide's own left, right and Keynesian
critiques are the ones to find real, named exemplars for. Output feeds T-0062.
Depends on T-0060 only for shared columns.
