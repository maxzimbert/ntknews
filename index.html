Four sections.
Every story.
Every morning.

In 1807, Jefferson described the ideal newspaper as one split into four sections: Truths, Probabilities, Possibilities and Lies. For two centuries the idea stayed a footnote. NTK runs every story through all four.

Truths
01

What is established and verifiable. The facts as they stand, and who they land on.

Probabilities
02

What happens next, read from historical pattern and political logic. A forecast that shows its reasoning.

Possibilities
03

Plausible outcomes that nobody is pricing in. The edge cases that would surprise a pundit but not a historian.

Lies
04

Deliberate distortions in circulation. NTK names the claim, names who is making it, and traces who profits.
