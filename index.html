The framework

Four sections.
Every story.
Every morning.

In 1807, Jefferson described the ideal newspaper as one split into four sections: Truths, Probabilities, Possibilities, and Lies. No editor ever built it. NTK did — and every story runs through all four.

Truth
01

What is factually established. Not framing, not narrative — what's actually known, and what it means.

Durable · Structural
Probabilities
02

What's likely, based on historical patterns and political logic. Grounded forecasts, not guesses.

Near-term · Pattern-based
Possibilities
03

Plausible but overlooked outcomes. The edge cases that would surprise a pundit but not a historian.

Black swans · Second-order
Lies
04

Deliberate distortions in circulation. NTK names the claim, names who's making it, traces who benefits.

Named actors · Exposed
