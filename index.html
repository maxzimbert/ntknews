The origin

Thomas Jefferson
hated the news.
He also designed NTK.

He had his reasons. A pamphleteer Jefferson once paid to attack John Adams had turned his pen on Jefferson himself, and by his second term the president had decided the papers were beyond saving. So when a young man wrote asking how to run a good one, Jefferson told him what he really thought:

"The man who never looks into a newspaper is better informed than he who reads them."

Then, in the same letter, he described the newspaper that might fix it. Four chapters: Truths. Probabilities. Possibilities. Lies. The first chapter, he warned, would be very short.

It stayed a footnote for two centuries. Until now.
